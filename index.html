PPD-Adapt is a research prototype about postpartum depression (PPD), which can affect mothers after childbirth. It asks a few thoughtful questions about your mood, anxiety, stress, sleep, and bonding with your baby, and adapts to your answers as you go. It's quick, private, and designed to support your well-being. It is not a diagnosis.

About this build

PPD-Adapt is an adaptive screening prototype. Instead of a fixed, one-size-fits-all questionnaire, it uses an item-response-theory engine to pick the single most informative next question based on everything you've answered so far, which is why most sessions end well before every item has been asked.

Adaptive by design

Each answer updates a running estimate across five domains, and the engine stops early once it has enough information, or reaches the session limit.

Runs on your device

The engine, item bank, and scoring all run client-side in this page. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or shared with a server.

Honest about its limits

This is a screening prototype, not a diagnosis. A verified Urdu translation is not wired into this build yet, so English is shown by default.

Privacy

Everything in this screening runs inside your browser. You can verify this yourself by opening your browser's developer tools and watching the Network tab during a full session. No requests go out.

  • No server calls. No fetch, no analytics, no third-party scripts are loaded once this page is open.
  • Nothing is saved after you close the tab. This build keeps your session in memory only. Refreshing or closing the page clears it.
  • No accounts, no cookies. You don't sign in, and nothing identifying you is collected.
  • Safety check is local too. The safety questions are scored on-device; if a concern is flagged, the guidance shown is generic and not sent anywhere.

This is a screening prototype, not a diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

Help

How does it choose which question to ask next?

After every answer, the engine re-estimates your responses across five domains, then picks the next informative question. It adapts the session while asking at least one question in every domain, and stops early once each domain is estimated with enough confidence, with a 30-question maximum.

Why did I see a safety question first?

A short set of safety-related questions always runs before the main questionnaire. If any answer suggests risk of self-harm, or of harming your baby, a full-screen message with guidance is shown before anything else continues.

What do I do with my results?

Treat the summary as a starting point for a conversation with a doctor, midwife, or therapist, not as a diagnosis on its own.

Is my language supported?

Right now every item is shown in English. An Urdu toggle exists, but this build doesn't yet include a verified Urdu translation for these items, so it falls back to English rather than risk a machine translation of a clinical question.
